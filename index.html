Government Schemes for FPOs

Key programmes and policies supporting Farmer Producer Organisations across India

🏦
Formation & Promotion of 10,000 FPOs
Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare

Central government scheme to form and promote 10,000 new FPOs by 2027–28 with ₹6,865 crore outlay and equity grants per FPO.

Learn more →
💰
NABARD FPO Financing
NABARD

Dedicated credit guarantee fund and concessional finance for FPOs including working capital loans, term loans, and capacity building grants.

Learn more →
📦
PM Kisan SAMPADA Yojana
Ministry of Food Processing

Grants for post-harvest infrastructure including cold chains, warehouses, and processing units for agri-produce handled by FPOs.

Learn more →
🌐
eNAM Integration for FPOs
Ministry of Agriculture

FPOs can onboard directly onto the National Agriculture Market (eNAM) platform to sell produce to buyers across India without middlemen.

Learn more →
🌱
SFAC Equity Grant Scheme
Small Farmers' Agribusiness Consortium

Matching equity grants up to ₹15 lakh per FPO and credit guarantee coverage to help FPOs access institutional credit more easily.

Learn more →
🎓
Capacity Building for FPOs
DAY-NRLM / Ministry of Rural Development

Training programmes on governance, financial management, digital literacy, and market linkage for FPO leaders and board members.

Learn more →
